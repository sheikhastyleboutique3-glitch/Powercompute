Each proposal has a fixed voting window. Your vote weight is your currently staked $PWR balance at the moment you vote (not a historical snapshot). A proposal succeeds if total votes cast meet the quorum threshold AND "For" votes exceed "Against" votes by the time voting closes. Anyone with at least the proposal threshold of staked $PWR can create a new proposal from the Admin Console (team-managed for now; a public proposal form may open up in a future release).
