Recommended setup: deploy a Gnosis Safe multisig (2-of-3 or 3-of-5) at app.safe.global, deploy PowerComputeTimelock with that Safe as its admin, then transfer ownership of PowerComputeToken / NodeRegistry / PowerComputePresale / PowerComputeAnnouncements to the Timelock address (via the Settings tab). From then on, every sensitive owner-only call on those contracts must be queued here and wait out the delay before it can execute — giving your community visibility and a chance to react before any privileged action takes effect.
