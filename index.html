How It Works

Three verifiable steps turn wasted electrons into decentralized AI compute.

STEP 01

Grid Curtailment Detection

Oracle-fed sensors identify stranded/curtailed renewable energy at wind & solar sites in real time, before it's wasted or throttled by the grid.

STEP 02

Node Verification (PoEC)

GPU node operators submit Proof-of-Energy-Consumption attestations, cryptographically verified on-chain against real-time energy meter data.

STEP 03

AI Compute Orchestration

Verified green capacity is routed to AI training/inference jobs via the PowerCompute marketplace, and operators are rewarded in $PWR.
